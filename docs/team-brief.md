# Team Brief: No Fate But What We Make

**Program:** one flagship session, one half-day stream, one starter repo, one recurring forum. The goal: get the whole team productive with agentic coding tools, from grads to veterans, with a 30-minute on-ramp and a deep end worth diving into.

**The universe:**

| Artefact | What it is |
|---|---|
| **A Day on the Tools with Lamb Dog** | 60-min flagship. My real working day, live: agent salvos, multi-tenant plumbing, Kimble hacks, review discipline. |
| **Judgement Day** | Half-day open workshop stream, T+3 weeks. Drop-in blocks. You vote via the kill list, I build it live. Tagline: "Come with me if you want to ship." |
| **clothes-boots-motorcycle** | The goodie-bag repo. Arrive with nothing, leave equipped. Tiered Day One / Week One / Deep End. |
| **TryNet** | Fortnightly 30-min office hours, ongoing. Like Skynet, but you try things and nobody gets terminated. |

---

## Phases

**Phase 0: Launch (T-3 weeks)**
- Announcement posted, flagship booked, recording on
- Kill list opens: nominate anything real to be built, fixed, or torn apart live at Judgement Day
- Repo skeleton created (private), demo assets staged

**Phase 1: Build and rehearse (T-2 to T-1 weeks)**
- Two full dry runs; the second with a live guinea pig from this team (volunteer wanted)
- Data-hygiene leak hunt on both recordings; every leak fixed structurally
- Pre-records cut (Kimble pipeline, voice demo, bootstrap timelapse)
- Goodie bag finished and flipped internal-visible; teasers posted

**Phase 2: Flagship (T-0)**
- 60 minutes, seven segments, hard time boxes
- Agents fired in minute 5, harvested in minute 50; the structure is the lesson
- Chat wingman needed on the day (volunteer wanted)

**Phase 3: Conversion (T+1 day to T+2 weeks)**
- Chaptered recording, follow-up post, promo clips
- Homework issued: Day One tier of the repo, 30 minutes, that night
- Kill list triaged; Judgement Day winners locked and pre-staged

**Phase 4: Judgement Day (T+3 weeks)**
- 10:00-15:00, drop-in blocks: Genisys (setup from zero), Skynet Core (deep plumbing), Rise of the Machines (lunch lurk), The Kill List, Dark Fate (bring your broken thing), Hasta La Vista (wrap)
- Live, unrehearsed coding on your voted targets; chat wrangler needed (volunteer wanted)

**Phase 5: TryNet steady state (ongoing)**
- Fortnightly, 30 min, recurring invite goes out at T+1 day
- Format: no agenda, no prep, bring setup problems, use-case questions, skill ideas, war stories; lurking is fine, I'll be building something on screen regardless
- Standing agenda hooks: goodie-bag FAQ additions, Mad Skills PR showcases, new plugin finds
- Review at the 3-month mark: attendance, questions logged, skills contributed; adjust cadence or kill it honestly if it's not earning its slot

## What I need from you

1. **Vote the kill list** (link in the announcement). Seeded already; make it yours.
2. **One dry-run guinea pig** (T-1 week, 75 min, ideally someone newer to the tooling).
3. **One chat wingman** for the flagship, **one chat wrangler** for Judgement Day.
4. **Bring a broken thing** to the Dark Fate block: a real problem we solve together on stream.
5. **PR a skill** into Mad Skills. First contributor gets bragging rights at Judgement Day.

## How we'll know it worked

- Day One tier installs within 48 hours of the flagship
- Kill list submissions and votes before close
- Mad Skills PRs from people who aren't me
- TryNet attendance holding after week 6, which is when novelty dies and habit either exists or doesn't
